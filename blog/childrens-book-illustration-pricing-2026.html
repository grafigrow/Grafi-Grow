Children's Book Illustration Pricing in 2026 — What $100 for 20 Images Covers

"How much does it cost to illustrate a children's book?" is the first question almost every self-published author asks me. It is also the hardest to answer in one number, because a book can be 12 pages or 32 pages, one character or ten, simple or very detailed.

So let me answer it plainly, with my own price first.

At GrafiGrow Studio, my starter price is $100 for 20 images. AI image-making is easy these days — what you are paying for is not one image, it is twenty images that look like they belong in the same book.

Here is exactly what that covers, and what it does not.

What $100 for 20 images includes

1. One consistent style. Soft watercolour with fine ink lines, warm natural light, rounded childlike faces, gentle storybook mood. No text in the images — you add the title and credits yourself, in your own lettering.

2. Character sheets first. Before the scenes, I make reference sheets for your main characters: front and three-quarter views, plus expression close-ups, on a plain background. You approve faces, clothes, colours, and ages before I draw the book. This is the step that stops characters changing from page to page.

3. One sample spread, then the book in story order. You see one finished spread from your own manuscript first. If the style fits, I continue. If it does not, we fix it then — not after twenty pages.

4. Print-ready files. Final images upscaled and delivered print-ready at 300 DPI, in the aspect ratio your book needs — usually square for inside pages, and a portrait cover. Formatted for Amazon KDP and IngramSpark, with bleed where your trim size requires it.

5. Consistency checks. Every image is checked against the cast list — hair, clothes, glasses, skin tone, height — and against any colour or story timeline in your book, like a tree turning from grey to green.

That is $5 per image. It is a budget starter price for short books and self-published authors, and I am honest about that.

What it does not include

It does not include a full 32-page book with a cover, back cover, and spine for $100. A standard 32-page picture book is usually 24 to 32 illustrations plus a cover, and it takes more time, more characters, and more fixes.

It does not include hand-painted original watercolour art, unlimited revisions, or a full book layout with text placed on every page. Text placement and book design are a separate service — tell me if you need it, and I will quote it separately.

And it does not include commercial work where you need full buy-out rights for a large publisher. For self-published KDP and IngramSpark books, standard use is included. For anything else, ask first.

How this compares to the wider market in 2026

For context, professional children's book illustrators often charge $100 to $500 per illustration, and a full 32-page book can cost $2,000 to $12,000 from an established pro. That is real, and that is fair for hand-drawn work from an illustrator with many published books.

My price is lower because the work is AI-assisted and human-directed. AI generates the images quickly; I direct the characters, style, fixes, and quality checks. That keeps the cost down for authors who could not otherwise afford a full book — and it means I will not claim the work is hand-drawn when it is not.

If a job says "No AI", I will not apply for it as hand-drawn work. That matters, and I would rather lose a job than mislead an author.

How to use this price

  • Short book, 20 images or fewer: $100 is a good fit. Send the manuscript and your trim size, and I can confirm the price.
  • Full book, 24 to 32 pages: ask for a quote. It will be more than $100, and I will tell you the exact number before I start.
  • Cover only, or character design only: also quotable separately. A cover is often the hardest single image in a book, because it has to sell the story in one picture.

Three questions that change the price

  1. How many characters? One child and a grandmother is simpler than eight children and three adults.
  2. How many pages? More pages means more images, more checks, and more time.
  3. What trim size and format? KDP paperback, hardcover, and IngramSpark all have different bleed and spine rules. Tell me the size up front — for example, 8.5 x 8.5 inches — so the files are right the first time.

Send those three answers with your manuscript, and I can give you a firm price and a timeline in one reply — usually 7 to 10 days for a short book after the sample spread is approved.

The short version

$100 for 20 images buys you a short, consistent, print-ready picture book in a soft watercolour style, AI-assisted and human-directed, with character sheets and a sample spread first. It is not a $2,000 hand-painted book, and I will not pretend it is. For many self-published authors, it is exactly enough to get their story into a child's hands.

If that sounds like your book, reach out through GrafiGrow Studio with your manuscript and trim size, and I will confirm the price before we start.
